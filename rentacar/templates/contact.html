Before you write to us

To help us resolve your request faster, include your registered email, account role (customer or owner), and—if applicable—the booking or listing ID. Screenshots are welcome for technical issues.

For account deletion requests, email us from your registered address with the subject line Account Deletion Request. We will confirm identity before processing.

Frequently asked questions

How do I list my car on RentACar?

Register as an owner, complete your profile, and submit any required verification documents. Once approved, open the Owner Dashboard, add your vehicle details, upload photos, set pricing and availability, and publish your listing.

How long does owner verification take?

Our administrators typically review owner profiles within 24–48 business hours. You will see your verification status update in the dashboard once the review is complete.

How do bookings and payments work?

Customers submit booking requests with preferred dates and rental type. Owners approve or decline requests in the dashboard. Unless an online payment feature is explicitly offered, payment is arranged directly between the customer and owner after confirmation.

Can I cancel or change a booking?

Changes depend on the booking status and owner availability. Contact the other party through the details provided in your dashboard, or email support if you need assistance coordinating a change.

How is my personal data used?

We use your information to operate accounts, process bookings, verify owners, and improve the Platform. We do not sell personal data. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.
